# Demo vehicle images

Representative images retrieved through CarsXE on September 13, 2026 with
`validate=true`, `size=Medium`, and `license=ModifyCommercially`.

- `2022-audi-q5.webp`: CarsXE result from `file.kelleybluebookimages.com`, with
  `2022-Audi-Q5` in the source filename; visually inspected as an Audi Q5.
- `2021-toyota-tacoma.webp`: CarsXE result from `static.tcimg.net`, with
  `2021-Toyota-Tacoma` in the source filename; visually inspected as a Tacoma.

These bundled images keep the fictional demo offline. They are representative
stock photos, not images of a customer's particular vehicle. Customer uploads
are private and are never committed here. The live app obtains stock image
bytes through its authenticated backend; provider keys are never in the app.
